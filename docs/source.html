What makes a record useful?

Describe the kind of evidence
Keep publisher statements, direct observations, proposals, and disputed claims distinguishable. Include the date, the source, and any conditions that limit the result.
Keep changes traceable
Link a correction to the earlier version and show why it changed. Keep public references separate from restricted material.
Test the backup
Copies under independent control reduce the chance that one mistake or outage harms them all. A manifest helps check file integrity during a restore. Assess factual accuracy by reviewing the claim and its evidence. LOCKSS preservation principles · BagIt file packaging

What can I access today?
The example records and public project notes are available here. The sample uses public sources and is designed to open in a spreadsheet or text editor.
How is this different from NuMind?
SOURCE records evidence and corrections. NuMind uses reviewed evidence to prepare readable guides and learning material.
Can I suggest a source or correction?
Use the NuTerra participation links. Include the relevant record or page, a public citation, and what the source supports.
