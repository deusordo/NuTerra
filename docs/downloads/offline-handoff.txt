NUTERRA OFFLINE HANDOFF CHECKLIST
Edition 1 · November 2026

Purpose
Find out whether another person can complete a low-risk job using a
saved or printed guide. A shared workshop inventory is one example.

Prepare
[ ] Pick one task, a safe place to try it, and an expected result.
[ ] Put the edition, publication date, and evidence references on the guide.
[ ] List required materials and where to find them.
[ ] Save the guide and approved attachments locally. Print a copy if useful.
[ ] Confirm the test materials can be shared with the reader.

Run
[ ] Open the files in a fresh local session and disconnect from the internet.
[ ] Ask someone unfamiliar with the guide to perform the task.
[ ] Observe where they look, what they ask, and where they stop.
[ ] Confirm the result against the expected outcome.
[ ] Record time, missing information, unexpected dependencies, and fixes.

Review
[ ] Update the guide and label the new version.
[ ] Keep the previous version with its evidence and corrections.
[ ] Have a second reader repeat the task before marking the handoff verified.

Use a safe, reversible test task. Store result notes without identifying
participants or recording private locations if the notes will be shared.
