Choose equipment you can maintain

Start with a job and a test. Use what you already have. For a one-time purchase, check replaceable batteries, common fittings, available spares, and whether it still works when the vendor’s cloud is unreachable. Look up the manual before paying and test the return window and offline behavior.

Compare lifetime cost. Add purchase price, ordinary service, consumables, and expected replacements. Divide by the years you expect to use it. An $80 tool with $40 in upkeep over six years averages $20 per year in this example. Replace the assumptions with dated local prices and documented service life.

The starter pack contains NuTerra’s guides, diagrams, worksheets, and offline readiness checklist. Open the included index first. App installers, map regions, and external reference collections can be saved from their publishers.

Prepare an offline library and maps

Kiwix

Free · Open source

Install the reader and save only the ZIM collections you need. Keep the edition and size in the index. Open the files once in airplane mode and test the search terms people will use.

Kolibri

Free · Open source

Download the installer and import a small learning channel for guided practice and feedback. Check the channel’s reuse terms and keep a simpler PDF or plain-text copy of essential instructions.

Organic Maps

Free · Open source

Download the areas you use on each device. Verify a route with the phone offline and print the important points. Check official alerts for closures or evacuation instructions.

USGS topoView

Free download

Get U.S. maps in GeoPDF or image formats. Save a dated copy and print a local overview. Check whether roads, crossings, and services still exist before relying on them.

restic

Free · Open source

Back up an offline library to a separate drive and practice restoring a few files to a fresh folder. Keep the recovery instructions and key available in a protected location.

Build and repair

iFixit

Free guides · Tools and parts sold separately

Find the exact model before buying a part. Try the repair with the documented tools and keep the original manual. Confirm the content license before reproducing a guide.

FreeCAD

Free · Open source

Draft a replacement part, workshop layout, or mounting bracket. Save the editable drawing plus STEP or a dimensioned PDF so someone can inspect it with another tool.

Open Source Ecology

Open designs · Materials and training vary

Study a specific machine or component. Look for a recent build record, parts sources, tested limitations, and a service procedure. Price the actual build before choosing it.

Appropedia

Free · Open collaboration

Compare practical builds and repair methods. Look for measured results, local material substitutes, dated instructions, and the license on the specific page.

Plan power and water

PVWatts

Free web tool

Estimate seasonal solar production and download the monthly or hourly results while connected. Use it to compare sites and assumptions. Model backup, storage, and poor-weather performance separately.

System Advisor Model

Free desktop software · Open source

Evaluate solar and battery scenarios locally after obtaining the software and input data. Export the assumptions and results. Use measured loads and review regulated systems with qualified specialists.

CDC emergency water guidance

Free reference

Print the guidance and keep the source and date on your copy. Use it for emergency household planning and confirm local instructions during a real incident.

Communicate and organize help

FEMA household preparedness guide

Free PDF

Print the communications and preparedness pages your household needs. Keep private contacts in a protected copy and practice a check-in without the internet.

Meshtastic

Open-source software · Compatible hardware is a one-time purchase

Try it only after a basic paper plan and alert receiver are in place. Confirm legal local settings, range, battery life, and message delivery in a practice run. Keep independent ways to obtain official alerts and request help.

FEMA CERT training materials

Free training references

Use the public materials to find a local training program. Practice roles and follow the operating guidance of the organizations coordinating a real response.

NOAA Weather Radio

Free broadcasts · Receiver is a one-time purchase

Check U.S. coverage where the radio will sit. Look for a battery compartment, local alert programming, an audible alarm, and a manual you can save.

Food and long disruptions

USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning

Free downloadable chapters

Save the chapters that match the foods you grow. Follow a current tested recipe, equipment requirements, and altitude adjustments exactly.

ICRC essential services during prolonged armed conflict

Free PDF

Study how civilian water, sanitation, healthcare, and electricity services depend on each other. Use it to identify which local service failure would leave people without essential support.
