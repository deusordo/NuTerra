The Vision

Preserve what humanity knows.
Develop what humanity needs.
Build what future generations can inherit.

NuTerra is a long-term initiative to preserve human knowledge, advance technology, organize human capabilities, and establish resilient, self-sufficient communities.

Framework

Source collects information, NuMind organizes it into accessible knowledge, and VIO supplies tools to apply it. Deus Ordo organizes people to put ideas to work, and VATRA provides a setting to test how they work together in daily life. Results and lessons flow back into Source and NuMind. Each pillar can advance independently and support the others.

NuTerra’s proposed role is to coordinate strategy, shared standards, legal and ethical compliance, partnerships, and accountable leadership. The framework prioritizes voluntary participation, individual liberty, privacy, family autonomy, freedom of belief, and property rights. Governance and succession procedures are still being developed.

Roadmap

Phases can overlap. Land, financing, and operating decisions depend on evidence and approval.

In development

Establish the foundation

Develop the identity, website, and framework. Build Source’s information intake, expand VIO’s computing, establish NuMind’s archives, and grow Deus Ordo’s community capabilities.

Proposed

Research and demonstrate

Study sustainable operations, land, costs, regulations, and infrastructure. Use the findings to design a small VATRA demonstration site.

Long-term goal

Operate and replicate

Develop a functioning self-sufficient community, document results, replicate what works, and establish multigenerational succession.

Progress

Deus Ordo, VATRA, NuMind, and VIO have public sites describing their work and plans. This roadmap describes goals for the wider NuTerra ecosystem. Verified pilot results will be recorded here as they are available to share.

Research

Starting points for independent study of offline knowledge, locally maintainable tools, and community design.

  • Kiwix and Internet Archive for offline access and digital preservation.
  • Open Source Ecology and Global Ecovillage Network for practical technology and community learning.
  • Long Now Foundation for long-term stewardship and preservation.
