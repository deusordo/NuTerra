Framework

Source keeps track of information. NuMind makes it useful as learning material. VIO develops tools people can use to put that knowledge to work. DEUS ORDO brings people together to carry out projects. VATRA is the planned community where those ideas could be tested in daily life. Each pillar can advance on its own and share what it learns.

NuTerra’s proposed role is to coordinate strategy, shared standards, legal and ethical compliance, partnerships, and accountable leadership. The framework prioritizes voluntary participation, individual liberty, privacy, family autonomy, freedom of belief, and property rights. Governance and succession procedures are still being developed.

FamilyOS is designed to keep personal records and household tracking private, with sharing limited to what people choose to make available.

Featured project · Offline handoff

File restore tested · Independent handoff proposed

Pick a simple job such as updating the inventory for a shared workshop. The target is for a second person to finish it from a printed or saved guide, with the internet off and the author unavailable.

  1. Keep the sources, edition, and correction history with the guide. Open the SOURCE example.
  2. Use a one-page guide with clear steps and an expected result. See NuMind’s guide review project.
  3. Write down who maintains the equipment and where to find the information. Open the VATRA worksheet.
  4. Restore the files and open them on another device. Try VIO’s offline sample.
  5. Have two people test the handoff and record the questions they had. See the DEUS ORDO service pilot.

A reference-packet test confirmed that restored files opened offline and that a checksum caught a deliberately changed file. The next test is whether someone else can complete the job using the guide alone.

Roadmap

Phases can overlap. Land, financing, and operating decisions depend on evidence and approval.

In development

Establish the foundation

Develop the identity, website, and framework. Build Source’s information intake, expand VIO’s computing, establish NuMind’s archives, and grow Deus Ordo’s community capabilities.

Proposed

Research and demonstrate

Study sustainable operations, land, costs, regulations, and infrastructure. Use the findings to design a small VATRA demonstration site.

Long-term goal

Operate and replicate

Develop a functioning self-sufficient community, document results, replicate what works, and establish multigenerational succession.

Research

Save a copy and prove it can be restored
Downloaded collections work when you have the file and a compatible reader. Keep a local copy and check that someone can open it after an outage. Kiwix Reader · NDSA Levels of Digital Preservation
Distinguish plans from field results
Detailed designs can reveal dependencies early. A land or infrastructure decision also needs site-specific costs, approvals, and operating evidence. One Community’s progress notes · Open Source Ecology development
Design for the next maintainer
A prototype helps uncover assumptions and training needs. A handoff also needs a named maintainer and instructions the next person can use. Long Now Clock FAQ
